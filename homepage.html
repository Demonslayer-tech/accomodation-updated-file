How It Works

1

Apply

Click “Apply Now” on your dashboard to begin your accommodation application for the 2026/2027 session.

2

Read the Instructions

Review the important information about how the process works, what the allocation is based on, and what you’ll need before continuing. Click Proceed when you’re ready.

3

Get Your Hostel

The system randomly allocates a hostel to you, like a ballot. The selection is fully automated, so you can't choose or change it.

4

Payment

After you're allocated, you'll be asked to proceed to payment. Pay within the given period (7 days) to keep your allocation.

5

Allocation Confirmed

Once your payment is received, your allocation is confirmed and complete. You can view your hostel details on your dashboard.
